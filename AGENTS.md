# Validata website design work

Read `DESIGN.md` before changing the visual experience. Apply the Apple Design skill at `~/.codex/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md` when available; the source is https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill. Read its relevant references before citing a guideline. Apply web foundations and interaction principles, rather than native Apple navigation conventions.

Preserve the original `src/assets/logo.png` and the established blue, black, and white identity. Motion must explain the product or respond to the visitor. Keep normal scrolling and equivalent content available on small screens and with reduced motion. Use clearly labeled illustrative data; don't invent product results.

Verify changed interactions with keyboard and pointer, phone and desktop widths, reduced motion, and the production build. Keep the existing Firebase preview workflow for design review.
